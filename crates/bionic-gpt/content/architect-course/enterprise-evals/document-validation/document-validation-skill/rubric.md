# Procurement Security Rubric

Assess the vendor package against these requirements:

| ID | Requirement | Passing evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | Data residency | Customer data remains in the European Economic Area. |
| R2 | Subprocessor control | New subprocessors require prior written customer approval. |
| R3 | Incident notice | Confirmed security incidents are reported within 48 hours. |
| R4 | Availability | The service commits to at least 99.9% monthly availability. |
| R5 | Service remedy | Availability failure provides a defined service credit. |
| R6 | Audit rights | The customer has a contractual audit or equivalent assurance right. |
| R7 | Deletion | Customer data is deleted or returned within 30 days after termination. |
| R8 | Liability | Liability protection covers confidentiality and data-protection breaches. |
| R9 | Termination | Material breach can be terminated after a cure period of no more than 30 days. |

Report a requirement as Unknown when the package does not establish it.
