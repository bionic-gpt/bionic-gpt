# Vendor Security Addendum

**Supplier:** Northstar Analytics Ltd.
**Customer:** Acme Retail Group
**Effective date:** 1 January 2026

## Data handling

The supplier may process customer data only to provide the contracted analytics
service. Customer data must remain in the European Economic Area. The supplier
must obtain written approval before appointing a new subprocesser.

## Security incidents

The supplier must notify the customer of a confirmed security incident without
undue delay and no later than 48 hours after confirmation. The notice must
describe the affected systems, likely impact, and remediation steps.

## Availability and service credits

The service commitment is 99.5% monthly availability. If availability falls
below that level, the customer may request a service credit equal to 10% of the
monthly subscription fee for the affected month.

## Liability and termination

The supplier's aggregate liability is limited to fees paid in the previous six
months, except for confidentiality breaches, data protection violations, and
fraud. Either party may terminate for material breach if the breach is not
cured within 30 days of written notice.

The addendum does not specify a customer audit right or a data-deletion period
after termination.
